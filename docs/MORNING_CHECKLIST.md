## Detailed Step-by-Step Instructions

### Minute 1: Email Digest Scan
1. Open `mail.google.com`.
2. Find the automated report from `ziauldin371@gmail.com` with subject:
   `[Growth System Agent] Run Report: COMPLETED (<Today's Date>)`
3. Scan key metrics:
   - **Discovered**: Number of new verified businesses found in current batch.
   - **Eligible for Email**: High-score leads with verified MX/syntax email.
   - **Manual Outreach Needed**: Leads with no public email but active Google Maps presence.

### Minute 2: Command Center Glance
1. Log into `https://growth-system-agent-brown.vercel.app`.
2. Look at the top banner:
   - ✅ **Green / Normal**: Next run scheduled for 17:00 PKT.
   - ⚠️ **Amber**: "Auto-send waiting for Gmail test" (go to Setup and send test email).
   - ⛔ **Red**: "Scheduler stopped" (GitHub Actions didn't run within 14h; check Actions tab).

### Minute 3: Review Prospect Replies
1. Click **Emails** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the **Replies** sub-tab.
3. If an interested business owner has replied, respond immediately with a calendar booking link or tailored technical answer.

### Minute 4: High-Value Manual Outreach
1. Click **Contact Manually** in the left sidebar.
2. These are high-opportunity businesses (e.g. 4.8 stars with 200+ reviews, but zero website link or broken social page).
3. For each business:
   - Check the **TPS Notice** (if in UK, verify Telephone Preference Service rules).
   - Click **Google Maps** to see their real listing and customer reviews.
   - Click **Copy Pitch** to grab the AI-drafted message backed 100% by live facts.
   - Reach out via Phone, WhatsApp, or Google Business profile.
   - Click **Mark as Contacted** to log your contact and prevent team duplication.

### Minute 5: Deliverability Health Check
1. Click **Safety** in the left sidebar.
2. Confirm:
   - **Bounce Rate**: Below 3% (healthy).
   - **Daily Limit**: On pace (e.g., 10–25 emails today during warm-up).
   - **Emergency Stop**: Disarmed / Normal.
